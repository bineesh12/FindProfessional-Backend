Request account deletion

What happens next

  • We may ask for limited information to verify that the account belongs to you.
  • After verification, we will delete or anonymise account information and associated marketplace data that is not required to be retained.
  • Data required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial records or other legal obligations may be retained for the applicable period.
  • Protected backups may take additional time to expire through their normal retention cycle.

Delete specific content

You may also ask to delete specific content without closing the entire account. Explain what should be removed in your email and we will review the request.
