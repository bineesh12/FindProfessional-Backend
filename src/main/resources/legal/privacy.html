Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Arbio connects customers with local professionals. This policy explains what personal data Arbio processes, why it is needed, who may receive it, and the choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Arbio is operated by the developer identified in the app-store listing. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@getarbio.com.

2. Data we process

Depending on how you use Arbio, we may process:

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, authentication identifiers, selected role, profile photo and verification status.
  • Professional profile data: business details, organisation number, service areas, offered services, portfolio descriptions and images.
  • Request and project data: descriptions, questionnaire answers, budgets, preferred dates, municipality, postcode and coordinates derived from a postcode.
  • Marketplace activity: opportunities viewed or declined, offers, attachments, hiring decisions, completion status, ratings and reviews.
  • Communications: conversations, messages and related delivery or read status.
  • Device and technical data: push-notification tokens, app installation identifiers, device information, IP address, security events and service logs.
  • Analytics data: app interactions and diagnostic information when you enable optional analytics.
  • Purchase data: subscription product, platform, entitlement status and transaction reference. Payment-card details are handled by the relevant app store and are not stored by Arbio.

3. How we use data

We process data to authenticate users, maintain profiles, create structured service requests, match relevant professionals, support offers and messaging, deliver notifications, verify subscriptions, prevent abuse, secure the service, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

When request-assistance features are used, relevant request text may be sent to an AI service to classify the need, identify missing information or draft a clearer description. AI output is validated by Arbio and does not make provider-selection, payment or authorization decisions.

4. Legal bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide the marketplace and requested features.
  • Consent: optional analytics and other processing where consent is requested. Consent can be withdrawn in the app.
  • Legitimate interests: service security, fraud prevention, reliability and support, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations: records or disclosures required by applicable law.

5. When data is shared

Request details and the location needed to assess a job may be shown to suitable professionals. Customers may see professional profile, service, verification, offer and review information. Direct contact details should only be shared when needed for the service.

We also use service providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, analytics when enabled, and push notifications.
  • Railway and PostgreSQL infrastructure for application hosting and data storage.
  • OpenAI for request-understanding features when those features are used.
  • OpenStreetMap/Nominatim for postcode-based location resolution.
  • Google Play or Apple for subscription purchase and entitlement verification.

We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party behavioural advertising.

6. International processing

Some providers may process data outside your country or the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate transfer safeguards are used. Firebase Authentication, for example, is operated from infrastructure in the United States.

7. Retention

Account and marketplace data is retained while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type. Data may remain temporarily in protected backups after deletion.

8. Your choices and rights

You can update profile information and analytics consent in the app. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal data. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Requests can be sent to info@getarbio.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Arbio account and associated data. Some records may be retained where required for security, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

Read the account-deletion instructions.

10. Security

Arbio uses HTTPS, access controls, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens and provider security controls. No service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect data proportionately to its sensitivity.

11. Children

Arbio is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

12. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will remain available at this URL.
